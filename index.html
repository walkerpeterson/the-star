Sources & notes

Facts on the Roanoke Star, El Paso's Star on the Mountain, and Palmanova drawn from Wikipedia, the World Record Academy, and Visit Roanoke VA. No official body tracks "largest non-illuminated man-made star-shaped structure" — Palmanova is the strongest documented candidate, not a certified record holder. Stellar radii for hypergiants like Stephenson 2-18 and UY Scuti are genuinely disputed in the astronomical literature.
